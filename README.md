NoteBud is an automated audio-notetaking application that listens to YouTube videos through your microphone, generates comprehensive summaries, and offers an intelligent file organizer to keep your study materials structured.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **AI Video Listening:** Captures and summarizes audio from YouTube videos playing out loud.
* **Smart Note Organizer:** Cleans, structures, and exports uploaded rough notes into polished files.
* **AI Study Mode Toggle:** Configurable settings to switch between exhaustive notes or high-level study tips with topic-specific resources.

## ⚠️ Important Requirements
* **No Headphones Allowed:** The application utilizes your system's hardware microphone to listen to your speakers. It will not capture audio if headphones are plugged in.

## 🛠️ How It Works

### 1. YouTube Audio Summarization
1. Open NoteBud and hit the **record** option.
2. Play your target YouTube video through your device speakers.
3. Once the video finishes, NoteBud processes the mic input and generates structured notes.

### 2. Organize My Notes
1. Click the **Organize My Notes** button.
2. Upload your raw, messy text files or previous note drafts.
3. Receive a cleanly formatted, beautifully organized download file.

### 3. Settings & Study Mode
Toggle **Study Tips** to customize your output:
* **Study Tips [OFF]:** Generates standard, highly detailed, verbatim text notes.
* **Study Tips [ON]:** Generates lighter notes, focusing instead on active recall strategies and actionable study tips.
* **Dynamic Recommendations:** With this mode on, the built-in AI automatically analyzes the video's subject matter and appends 5 curated external websites directly relevant to that specific topic to help you study further.
